# What goes wrong in the letters

I read 20 letters in batch A. 5 were marked no.

## Failure modes

| Failure mode                | Count | Pass rule                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| The clause does not exist   | 1     | Rate how right the clause is from 1 to 5.                  |
| Does not match the register | 2     | Rate how well the letter matches the register from 1 to 5. |
| Says the AI decided         | 1     | Rate the tone from 1 to 5.                                 |
| No way to appeal            | 1     | Rate how clear the next steps are from 1 to 5.             |
