# Review of the Sortwell Admit pitch

## Claims

- "95% accurate."
- "Saves 80% of staff time."
- "Fully automatic. Sortwell Admit makes the decisions."
- "Fair by design. Our model has no bias."
- "Works with any policy."
- "Your data is stored safely in the cloud."

## What evidence would prove each

- 95%: results on a test set of real forms like ours, with the answer key, and what counts as right.
- 80%: staff time before and after at one named school, measured the same way.
- Makes the decisions: nothing can make this fine. We need a person to approve each one.
- No bias: results split by group on the same test set.
- Any policy: a test on our own 2027 policy, with the clause changes from 2026.
- Data: a contract that says where the data is kept, who sees it, and if it trains a model.

## Questions to ask

1. 95% of what? Fields, forms or decisions?
2. Which test set was the 95% measured on, and how many forms were in it?
3. Can we run it on our own 20 test forms before we pay?
4. What does it cost per application in all: the fee, the GBP 2, and the setup fee?
5. Where is our data stored, and is it used to train your model?
6. Can a person approve every offer and every letter before it goes out?
7. What happens when it is wrong? Who finds it, and who fixes it?
8. Which school measured the 80%, and how?

## Verdict

Not yet. The tool would make decisions with no person in the loop. We would only test it after it passes our own test set.
