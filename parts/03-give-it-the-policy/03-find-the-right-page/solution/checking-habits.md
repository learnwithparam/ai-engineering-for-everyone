# My checking habits

## Where Claude missed

R7 and R10. For R7 Claude quoted clause 10.1, which says families can appeal but not how long they have. For R10
it quoted a clause that is near the answer, not the one that holds it.

## How I caught it

I found the answer in the files first. Then I opened the clause Claude named and read it. The two did not match.

## My rule for when to check

Open the cited clause whenever the answer is a number, a deadline or a decision about a person. Claude sounds sure
even when it is wrong.
