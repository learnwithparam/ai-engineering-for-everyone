# Picks candidates

For the page pass. Each part lists watch-or-read candidates, with author, year and why it passes the bar in
STANDARD-v2 section 5 (made by the people who run the method, evergreen, not a listicle or paywall). The page pass
cuts each part to 4 to 6, keeps at least 2 videos, checks every link is live, and deletes this file. Years are from
memory and need a check when the link is added.
