# Harbour Hill School: How to appeal a decision

Fictional. Harbour Hill School, Kestrel Bay, and every person in this folder are made up for a course.

A1. If your child was not offered a place, you can appeal. An appeal panel that is not part of the school hears
it.

A2. To appeal, fill in the appeal form from the Kestrel Bay Council website and send it to the clerk to the
appeal panel within 20 school days of the date on your decision letter.

A3. Say why you think your child should have a place. You can send letters or other papers that help.

A4. The panel looks at two things. First, did the school follow its admissions policy and the law? Second, does
your child's need for a place outweigh the problems one more child would cause the school?

A5. You will be told the time and place of the hearing at least ten school days before it. You can bring a
friend or someone to speak for you.

A6. The panel's decision is final for the school. If you think the panel made a mistake in how it worked, you
can complain to the council.

A7. Your child stays on the waiting list while you appeal. Appealing does not change their position.
