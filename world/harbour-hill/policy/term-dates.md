# Harbour Hill School: Key dates for families joining Year 7

Fictional. Harbour Hill School, Kestrel Bay, and every person in this folder are made up for a course.

T1. Applications close: 31 October 2026.

T2. Offers are sent to families: 1 March 2027.

T3. Families accept or turn down an offer by: 15 March 2027.

T4. Appeal forms must reach the clerk within 20 school days of the decision letter.

T5. New Year 7 open evening: 24 June 2027.

T6. Autumn term starts: 6 September 2027. The waiting list closes when the autumn term ends.
